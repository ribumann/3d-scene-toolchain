Changes to each photograph: EXIF orientation normalized, converted to RGB,
entire frame downsampled to a maximum 1800-pixel side with Pillow LANCZOS,
metadata removed, and re-encoded as PNG. No object isolation, inpainting or
compositing. Exact dimensions and original/prepared SHA256 hashes are in
provenance.json. The originals remain available at the linked Commons pages.

The reconstruction experiment adds manually reviewed equipment boxes, SAM2
segmentation/cropping and Idol-generated object splats with inferred geometry.
Source-derived adaptations, including those crops and splats when published,
are distributed under CC BY-SA 4.0 with these credits and modification notices.
No original author endorses this experiment. Inventory and boxes are operator
supervision, not ground-truth geometry, dimensions or accuracy certification.
The images retain real pipework, overlap and background; R02 has an edge-cut
casing and R03 is a wider-context occlusion stress input, not an ideal capture.
